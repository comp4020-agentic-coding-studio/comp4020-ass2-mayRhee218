# Policies and support

## Late work and extensions

Written work is due at noon on its listed date. Work submitted within 48 hours
of the deadline is accepted with a 10% penalty per day; after that, contact
May directly rather than submitting late. Extensions are easy to arrange
in advance of a deadline --- email before it passes, not after.

## Academic integrity

Every essay must be your own argument, in your own words, with any assistance
(including generative AI) declared in a short note at the end of the
submission. Watching the assigned episodes is non-negotiable: an essay that
could have been written from a synopsis rather than the drama itself will be
asked to be resubmitted.

## Attendance

Screenings are discussion-based and don't repeat well from notes. If you miss
one, watch the drama on your own before the following week and email a short
note on what you took from it.

## A note on subject matter

Several weeks --- gender and sexuality (week 5), colonial-period history (week
8), materialism (week 10), and appearance and cosmetic surgery (week 11) ---
handle sensitive or contested material. These weeks are framed explicitly to
avoid stereotyping or reducing Korean society to a single claim; if you have
concerns about a specific week, raise them with May beforehand.

## Getting help

Email for anything that can wait; raise anything urgent in the screening
itself. See the [people page](/people/) for contact details.
