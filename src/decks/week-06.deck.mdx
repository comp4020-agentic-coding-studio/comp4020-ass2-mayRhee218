# Misaeng
## Pattern, or stereotype?

---

## Today's drama

**Misaeng (미생)** --- Incomplete Life --- a former professional Go player
turned contract worker at a trading company.

Often cited by Korean viewers as one of the more accurate workplace dramas
made, which makes it a useful test case for this week's real question.

---

## Three questions

1. What does Misaeng reveal about Korean corporate culture specifically,
   rather than corporate culture in general?
2. How do hierarchy, seniority, teamwork, and long working hours interact
   in the show's office?
3. How should we tell a social pattern apart from a stereotype, when a
   drama presents both with the same confidence?

---

---

## Take from this week

- hierarchy as a structuring feature of the workplace, not just a
  management style
- seniority and what it entitles someone to, formally and informally
- corporate culture as specific Korean institutions, not "how everyone
  works"
- the contract-versus-permanent distinction the plot turns on
