# Crash Landing on You
## A love story that needs the border to work

---

## Before you watch

- watch the assigned episodes
- bring one moment where the romance requires you to overlook something
  about the actual division
- a detail smoothed over so the story can proceed

---

## What we're testing

The drama has two jobs: representing a real, ongoing division, and telling a
love story that needs obstacles to overcome.

Most of the interesting discussion sits where those two jobs pull against
each other.

---

{/* _class: quote */}

> What does this get right about the border, and what does it invent to let
> the romance proceed?
