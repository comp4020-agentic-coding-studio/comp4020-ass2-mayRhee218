# When Life Gives You Tangerines
## One household, watched across the decades

---

## Today's drama

**Pokssak Sogatsuda (폭싹 속았수다)** --- a Jeju Island family from a hard
postwar childhood to the adulthood of the next generation.

The span is the point: we watch what "family" was expected to mean change
underneath people who didn't necessarily choose the change.

---

## Three questions

1. How have Korean family structures changed across the decades the drama
   covers?
2. How does economic development --- migration, new work, new money ---
   reshape family relationships?
3. How do different generations in the drama understand duty and
   independence differently from one another?

---

{/* _class: quote */}

> A single household lets us watch what "family" was expected to mean change
> underneath people who didn't choose the change themselves.

---

## Take from this week

- family as a structure with historically specific, changing expectations
- filial responsibility, and what it has and hasn't meant across
  generations
- generational difference as a source of both continuity and conflict
- how quickly a "traditional" norm can turn out to be recent
