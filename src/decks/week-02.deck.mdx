# KPop Demon Hunters
## What K-pop's visibility is actually made of

---

## Today's drama

**KPop Demon Hunters (케이팝 데몬 헌터스)** --- a girl-group act folded into a
monster-hunting premise.

K-pop is the most globally visible part of contemporary Korean culture. This
week asks what that visibility is made of.

---

## Three questions

1. Why has K-pop become globally influential, when so much national pop
   music doesn't travel?
2. How does Korean popular culture combine local identity with globally
   engineered production?
3. What does K-pop reveal about contemporary Korean identity, as opposed to
   what it's marketed as revealing?

---

{/* _class: quote */}

> What is K-pop's visibility actually made of? A production system, a
> fandom, and a set of choices about what gets exported.

---

## Take from this week

- K-pop as an industrial system, not a genre of song
- fandom as an organised, often transnational, labour of its own
- Hallyu / the Korean Wave, and what "wave" implies about direction and
  export
- cultural export as policy as much as taste
