# Jewel in the Palace
## A costume epic that behaves like a food-systems documentary

---

## Before you watch

- watch the assigned episodes
- bring one scene where food is doing more work than just being food
- a meal that marks who outranks whom, or a punishment that runs through the
  kitchen rather than around it

---

## What we're testing

We work scene by scene through the food system the drama depicts: royal
cuisine, medicine, and rank.

Every pass this semester asks the same two questions --- what does this get
right, and what does it invent.

---

{/* _class: quote */}

> The real Jang-geum appears in the historical record in a handful of lines.
> Everything else is the writers filling the gaps.

## After this screening

Find one gap in the record, and one thing the drama built to fill it --- the
habit this whole course will keep asking for.
