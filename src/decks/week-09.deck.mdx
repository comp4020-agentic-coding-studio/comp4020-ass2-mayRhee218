# Reply 1988
## A hard decade, remembered warmly

---

## Today's drama

**Reply 1988 (응답하라 1988)** --- five neighbouring Seoul families, in the
last years of the decade, foregrounding ordinary domestic life over the
politics happening around it.

That choice is itself worth examining.

---

## Three questions

1. What was ordinary life like under authoritarian rule, for people not
   directly involved in politics?
2. How did working-class and middle-class families experience rapid
   economic change in this period?
3. How does nostalgia affect historical memory --- what does a warm
   retelling make easier to forget?

---

{/* _class: quote */}

> A show can be historically situated in an authoritarian, rapidly
> developing country while barely mentioning either directly.

---

## Take from this week

- 1980s Korea as a period of both authoritarian rule and rapid growth,
  simultaneously
- democratisation, which the period is building toward without the show
  dwelling on it
- neighbourhood culture as a form of social organisation the show treats
  as central
- nostalgia, and what a fond memory of a hard decade tends to leave out
