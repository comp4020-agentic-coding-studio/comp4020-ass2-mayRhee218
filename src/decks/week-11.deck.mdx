# My ID Is Gangnam Beauty
## A live debate, not a settled fact

---

## Today's drama

**My ID Is Gangnam Beauty (내 ID는 강남미인)** --- a student who has undergone
extensive cosmetic surgery starts a new school, and the social response ---
praise and cruelty --- follows her there.

A deliberately uncomfortable premise, sitting inside a real, ongoing Korean
debate.

---

## Three questions

1. Why is appearance such a powerful social topic in contemporary Korea?
2. How are beauty standards constructed, and by whom --- media, family,
   peers?
3. How has Korean society itself debated cosmetic surgery, rather than
   simply accepted or rejected it?

---

{/* _class: quote */}

> Cosmetic surgery rates and attitudes vary considerably by age, region,
> and social group --- that debate is the actual subject this week, not a
> settled national fact.

---

## Take from this week

- beauty standards as constructed and historically specific, not natural
  or fixed
- appearance as a social and economic asset, not only a personal
  preference
- gender, and how differently appearance pressure lands depending on it
- media's role in setting a standard that then gets treated as natural
