# SKY Castle
## Satire that only works because the pressure is real

---

## Today's drama

**SKY Castle (스카이 캐슬)** --- wealthy families and the private admissions
coordinators they hire to get children into Korea's top three universities.

Exaggerated by design, but the exaggeration is a useful diagnostic.

---

## Three questions

1. Why is education so socially important in Korea, beyond "parents want
   their children to do well"?
2. What role does university entrance play in family life and family
   status?
3. What does SKY Castle exaggerate for satire, and what does it capture
   accurately about the pressure underneath?

---

{/* _class: quote */}

> It only works as satire because the underlying pressure is real and
> widely recognised.

---

## Take from this week

- education as a site where family status and a child's future are both
  at stake
- university admissions and the weight placed on a small number of
  institutions
- hagwon culture as an industry built around that weight
- inequality --- who can afford the coordinators the show centres, and who
  can't
