# KPop Demon Hunters
## A fantasy premise built on top of a real industry

---

## Before you watch

- watch the assigned episodes
- note one moment where the show seems to describe the real K-pop industry
  rather than invent a fantasy version of it

---

## What we're testing

The demon-hunting premise is a lens, not a distraction: what does it
exaggerate about K-pop, and what does the exaggeration make easier to see?

From there we move to the real system underneath --- training, choreography,
styling --- and the fandom built around it.

---
