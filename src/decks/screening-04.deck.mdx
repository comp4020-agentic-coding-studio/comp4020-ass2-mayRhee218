# When Life Gives You Tangerines
## The same household, three generations of expectation

---

## Before you watch

- watch the assigned episodes
- track one expectation --- about duty, marriage, or work --- that a parent
  holds and a child eventually doesn't

---

## What we're testing

We follow the household across its whole timeline rather than any single
episode, asking at each point what "family" was assumed to require, and who
bore the cost of meeting that requirement.

---
