# Mr. Sunshine
## A moving scene is not a historical source

---

## Before you watch

- watch the assigned episodes
- bring one scene you found moving, and ask of it: drawn from the record, or
  invented for the story?

---

## What we're testing

We hold the drama's two roles apart throughout: what it draws from the
historical record of colonial-period Korea, and what it invents or
romanticises for a nationalist story that plays well as entertainment.

---

{/* _class: quote */}

> Neither role disqualifies the other, but conflating them does.

---

{/* _class: banner */}

## After this screening

The habit of separating a source from a story is the one this whole course
asks you to bring to every drama --- this week just asks for it under the
highest stakes.
