## The brief

> Choose a thread that runs through at least two of this semester's dramas,
> and argue what putting them side by side actually shows.

Twelve weeks built a shared vocabulary --- hierarchy, representation versus
dramatisation, generational change, visibility, nostalgia --- that shows up
across very different dramas and subjects. This essay asks you to pick a
thread of your own and follow it across at least two weeks, arguing for what
the comparison reveals that neither drama shows on its own.

A strong essay does not treat its two dramas as two separate mini-essays glued
together. It states a comparative claim early, and every section of the
argument works for that claim specifically.

## What you submit

A 2000--2500 word essay comparing at least two dramas from the semester,
citing specific scenes as evidence and naming the course concepts it draws on.

## How it's marked

The comparative argument itself carries the most weight, followed by how
precisely the essay uses concepts from the course rather than generic film
commentary. Originality --- a thread we didn't hand you directly in
lecture --- and clear writing round out the criteria.
