## Before the screening

Watch the assigned episodes. Note one reaction to the main character --- kind or
cruel --- and what standard it's enforcing.

## In the screening

We use the show's premise to trace where beauty standards actually come
from --- media, peers, family --- and how social comparison keeps them in place,
holding off the stereotype this topic invites at every turn.

## Afterwards

Compare this week's construction of a standard to week 2's K-pop production
system --- both ask who builds an ideal, and for whom.
