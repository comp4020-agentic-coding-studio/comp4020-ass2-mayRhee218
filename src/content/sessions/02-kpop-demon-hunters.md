## Before the screening

Watch the assigned episodes. Note one moment where the show seems to be
describing the real K-pop industry rather than inventing a fantasy version of
it.

## In the screening

We use the show's demon-hunting premise as a lens rather than a distraction:
what does it exaggerate about K-pop, and what does the exaggeration make
easier to see? From there we move to the actual production system --- training,
choreography, styling --- and the fandom structures built around it.

## Afterwards

Keep your notes on "what gets exported and what doesn't" --- week 10 (materialism
and consumer culture) and week 11 (beauty standards) both return to versions of
this same question from different angles.
