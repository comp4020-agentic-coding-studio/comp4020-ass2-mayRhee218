## Before the screening

Watch the assigned episodes. Come with one scene in mind where food is doing
more work than just being food --- a meal that marks who outranks whom, or a
punishment that runs through the kitchen rather than around it.

## In the screening

We open on the show's premise, then work scene by scene through the food
system it depicts: royal cuisine, medicine, and rank. Each pass asks the same
two questions --- what does this get right, and what does it invent --- since
those two turn out to be a template we reuse most weeks this semester.

## Afterwards

The distinction between "represented" and "dramatised" you practise here is
the one your close-analysis essay later asks you to hold onto for a full
argument, on a week of your choosing.
