## Before the screening

Watch the assigned episodes. This week's discussion opens with a shared ground
rule, not a debate --- read the lecture's approach note before you arrive.

## In the screening

We treat the disguise premise as evidence of what a 2007 audience needed to
find plausible, rather than as a verdict on anyone's actual beliefs, then trace
what's changed and what hasn't since. Diversity of view within Korean society,
then and now, stays part of the discussion throughout.

## Afterwards

The same discipline --- separating what a drama represents from what a whole
society believes --- returns explicitly in weeks 10 and 11, on materialism and
beauty standards.
