## Before the screening

Watch the assigned episodes. Bring one moment where the romance plot requires
you to overlook something about the actual division --- a detail smoothed over
so the story can proceed.

## In the screening

We separate the drama's two jobs: representing a real, ongoing division, and
telling a love story that needs obstacles to overcome. Most of the interesting
discussion sits in where those two jobs pull against each other.

## Afterwards

This week is a strong candidate for your close-analysis essay if you're drawn
to the gap between what a drama documents and what it invents --- the brief for
that essay asks you to hold exactly this distinction for a full argument.
