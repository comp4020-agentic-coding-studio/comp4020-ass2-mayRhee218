## Before the screening

Watch the assigned episodes. Track one expectation --- about duty, marriage, or
work --- that a parent holds and a child eventually doesn't.

## In the screening

We follow the household across its timeline rather than any single episode,
asking at each point what "family" was assumed to require, and who bore the
cost of meeting that requirement.

## Afterwards

Keep this week's notes on generational change in view for week 7 (education)
and week 9 (1980s Korea) --- both return to family life under different
pressures.
