## Before the screening

Watch the assigned episodes. Bring one scene you found moving, and be ready to
ask of it: is this drawn from the historical record, or invented for the story?

## In the screening

We hold the drama's two roles apart throughout: what it draws from the
historical record of colonial-period Korea, and what it invents or
romanticises for a nationalist story that plays well as entertainment. Neither
role disqualifies the other, but conflating them does.

## Afterwards

The habit of separating a source from a story is the one this whole course
asks you to bring to every drama --- this week just asks for it under the
highest stakes.
