## Before the screening

Watch the assigned episodes. Note one scene that a Korean office worker might
recognise immediately, and one that feels exaggerated for drama.

## In the screening

We work through the office's hierarchy layer by layer --- seniority, contract
status, working hours --- then turn to the harder question of which parts are a
documented pattern in Korean workplace research and which are dramatic
shorthand.
