## Before the screening

Watch the assigned episodes. Note one moment where a real brand name or a
specific price does work that a vaguer reference wouldn't.

## In the screening

We start from the mechanics of the scam --- why it works, who falls for it --
before moving to the harder question of what that tells us about status and
visibility in the society the show is aimed at, resisting the easy
"materialistic society" read along the way.
