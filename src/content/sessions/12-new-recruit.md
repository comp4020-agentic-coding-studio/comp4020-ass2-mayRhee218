## Before the screening

Watch the assigned episodes. Bring one moment of barracks hierarchy that
reminded you of an institution from an earlier week.

## In the screening

Our last screening closes the semester's running thread on hierarchy by
tracing it through its most compressed, universal version --- conscription ---
and asking what a comic tone does to a mandatory, sometimes harsh institution.

## Afterwards

This is the last screening. Your final comparative essay, due at the end of
the teaching period, is where all twelve weeks' hierarchies --- court, corporate,
familial, military --- should start talking to each other.
