## Before the screening

Watch the assigned episodes. Bring one everyday detail --- food, money, a
neighbourhood ritual --- that tells you something about the decade the show
doesn't say outright.

## In the screening

We read the neighbourhood's daily life against the authoritarian, rapidly
developing state it sits inside, asking what a nostalgic retelling of a hard
decade tends to soften.

## Afterwards

Compare this week's family life to week 4's --- the same institution,
generations and one authoritarian decade apart.
